# IT Setup Guide for New Employees

This guide covers your laptop, accounts, passwords, VPN and support. Contact the IT Helpdesk at it-help@brightline.example or extension 4100.

## Your laptop

Every employee receives a laptop with the standard company image. Do not install software that is not on the approved list. To request extra software, open a ticket in the category SW-REQ and include a business reason. Your manager must approve the request.

## Accounts

Your Brightline ID is your first initial followed by your surname, for example jsmith. It gives you access to email, chat, the shared drive and the VPN. Your account is created before your start date and the temporary password is handed over on day 1 at the IT desk in Room 2B.

## Password and MFA rules

- Passwords must contain at least 14 characters.
- Passwords expire every 180 days and the last 10 passwords cannot be reused.
- Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for every account and uses an authenticator app on your phone.
- Staff in Finance and system administration must use a hardware security key instead of the app.

## VPN

The BrightLink VPN client is required whenever you work on a network that is not the office network, including home and public Wi-Fi. Sign in with your Brightline ID and your MFA code. Two common errors are listed below.

| Error code | Meaning | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| VPN-807 | Your device certificate has expired | Reinstall the VPN profile from the IT portal |
| VPN-612 | MFA code does not match | Re-enroll your authenticator through the IT Helpdesk |

## Printing

Printers release jobs when you tap your staff badge. The third-floor printer is PR-3F and the warehouse office printer is PR-WH.

## Support hours and priorities

The IT Helpdesk is open from 7.30 am to 6 pm on business days. Urgent faults can be reported by phone on extension 4100.

| Priority | Example | Target response |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Cannot work at all, or a security incident | 1 hour |
| P2 | Major tool broken, workaround exists | 4 hours |
| P3 | Minor issue or general question | 2 business days |

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone within 2 hours to security@brightline.example. IT will lock the device and wipe it remotely if needed.

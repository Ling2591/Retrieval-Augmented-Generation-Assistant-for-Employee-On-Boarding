## Ordering and shipping

- Orders placed before 2 pm ship the same day when the item is in stock.
- Orders above 250 USD ship free. Smaller orders pay a flat shipping fee of 12 USD.
- The lead time column shows business days for items that are not in stock.

## Returns

Customers may return unopened items within 30 days in the original packaging. Opened items can be returned within 30 days with a 15 percent restocking fee. Safety boots (ITM-10009) cannot be returned once worn.

## Customer discount tiers

- Silver tier is 5 percent off after 2,000 USD of spend in a calendar year.
- Gold tier is 8 percent off after 10,000 USD of spend in a calendar year.

Discounts do not apply to the pallet jack (ITM-10016).

## Shelving note

ITM-20417 and ITM-20418 have similar codes but are different products. ITM-20417 is the five tier shelving unit and ITM-20418 is only the extension kit, which cannot stand on its own.

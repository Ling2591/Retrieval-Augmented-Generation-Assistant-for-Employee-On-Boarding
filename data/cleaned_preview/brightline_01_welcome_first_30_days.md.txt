# Welcome to Brightline Supply Co.

Brightline Supply Co. is a distributor of packaging, workshop and warehouse supplies with about 60 employees. This guide explains what to expect during your first 30 days.

## Who to contact

- People Team, people@brightline.example, extension 4200
- IT Helpdesk, it-help@brightline.example, extension 4100
- Your manager and your onboarding buddy, who is assigned before your start date

Office hours are 8.30 am to 5.30 pm, Monday to Friday.

## Day 1

1. Collect your laptop from the IT desk in Room 2B before 9 am.
2. Sign your employment agreement with the People Team.
3. Complete the Safety Induction (course code SAF-100) before you enter the warehouse. Office staff who never visit the warehouse still complete it within the first week.
4. Meet your onboarding buddy and have lunch with your team. Lunch on day 1 is paid for by the company.

## Week 1

- Set up your accounts by following the IT Setup Guide.
- Finish two compliance modules. Data Protection Basics takes about 45 minutes and the Code of Conduct module takes about 30 minutes.
- Enroll in company benefits within 14 days of your start date. Missing this window means waiting until the next open enrollment period in January.
- Submit your bank details to the People Team for payroll.

## Days 2 to 30

- Hold a weekly one-to-one with your manager.
- Shadow at least two colleagues from other departments.
- Complete a 30-day check-in with the People Team on day 30.

## Probation

All new employees serve a probation period of 90 days. A formal probation review takes place around day 75, and your manager confirms the outcome in writing before day 90. During probation, either side can end the employment with 1 week of written notice. After probation the notice period is 4 weeks.

## Payroll

Salaries are paid on the 25th of each month. If the 25th falls on a weekend or public holiday, payment is made on the previous business day. Timesheets for hourly staff are due on the 20th.

# Leave Policy (effective 1 January 2026)

This policy replaces the Leave Policy dated 1 January 2024.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per year, accruing at about 1.67 days per month. Up to 7 unused days can be carried over, and carried over days must be used by 31 March of the following year. Leave of more than 3 consecutive days must be requested at least 10 business days in advance.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required after 2 consecutive days of absence. In addition, each employee receives 1 wellbeing day per year that does not need a reason.

## Parental leave

The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of paid leave and the secondary caregiver receives 4 weeks.

## Bereavement leave

Employees receive 5 days of paid leave on the death of an immediate family member and 2 days for an extended family member.

## Public holidays and volunteer day

The company observes 11 public holidays per year. Each employee also receives 1 paid volunteer day per year.

## Leave during probation

Unchanged from the previous policy. Employees in their first 90 days may use accrued leave, with manager approval, but not more than 5 consecutive days.
